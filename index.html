        Privacy and terms

        Last updated 30 July 2026

        What happens to your data

        Nothing you type here is uploaded. There is no account, no login, no database and no server belonging to this tool. Your entries, your resume and your settings are stored by your own browser, on this device, using local storage.

        That means two things worth knowing. Nobody, including the person who made this, can see your resume. And if you clear your browser data, or use a different device or browser, your work will not be there. Use Save to file to keep a copy you control.

        If you connect your own AI key

        This is optional and off by default. If you connect a key, your key and the entries you send go directly from your browser to the provider you chose, such as Anthropic or OpenAI, and are then covered by that provider's own privacy policy. They do not pass through any server owned by this tool, because there is not one. Your key is stored in your browser and you can remove it at any time with Disconnect.

        If you upload a file

        Your file is read inside your browser and is never uploaded anywhere. Plain text and Markdown are read directly. PDF and Word need a reader, so the first time you open one of those the page fetches the open-source pdf.js or mammoth.js library from a public CDN (cdnjs.cloudflare.com). Only the library comes down; your document never goes up.

        Analytics

        There are no analytics scripts, no cookies, no pixels, no fingerprinting and no third-party trackers on this page. The only outbound request made on load is to Google Fonts for the typeface. The hosting provider keeps standard server logs, as all web hosts do.

        Your rights

        Because no personal data is collected or held, there is nothing to request, export or delete from anybody else. You already hold all of it. Clearing your browser storage for this site erases it completely and immediately.
